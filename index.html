Why these are free

The clinical questionnaires in MindBaseline (PHQ‑9, GAD‑7, WHO‑5, Mini‑IPIP) are public‑domain instruments that exist so everyone can be screened. Putting a paywall between a struggling person and a screener would betray the instruments and the point. No servers, no accounts, no running costs — so there is nothing to recover.

The code is MIT‑licensed: use it, learn from it, fork it, ship it. The Rocaudio™ name and mark are not part of that grant — if you fork, please rename.

Disclaimer. MindBaseline is a screening and practice tool — not a medical device, not a diagnosis, and not an IQ test. Only a qualified professional can diagnose. If results concern you, or if you are having thoughts of harming yourself, contact a professional or your local emergency number; findahelpline.com lists free helplines in 130+ countries (US/CA: 988 · UK/IE: Samaritans 116 123 · AU: Lifeline 13 11 14).
Privacy. Every app runs entirely in your browser and stores data only in your own device’s local storage. Nothing is uploaded, tracked, or sold — there is no server to send anything to.
