What this is

Two honest check-ups: your mind, and your brain.

Mind uses real clinical instruments that doctors actually use — PHQ‑9 (depression), GAD‑7 (anxiety), WHO‑5 (wellbeing) — plus the Mini‑IPIP, a validated 20-item Big Five personality measure. Brain runs six timed cognitive tasks measuring working memory, processing speed, attention control, reasoning, numeracy and verbal ability. ~20 minutes total. Results are blunt: raw scores, standard bands, plain-language verdicts.

The honesty charter

What “direct and truthful” means here

1. Validated instruments, standard scoring. No invented “vibe quizzes” — the questionnaires are the published ones, scored the published way.

2. Plain results. Scores, bands, thresholds, and what to do about them. No sugar-coating, no mystical vagueness.

3. Honest limits. These are screeners, not diagnoses — only a professional can diagnose. And you will get no IQ number, because a real IQ test takes 1–2 hours with a trained examiner; any website that prints you an IQ is lying. What you get instead: honest per-skill numbers vs. typical adult ranges.

4. Private. Everything stays in this browser. Nothing is uploaded. Ever.
